Background

Hi, I'm Kerry, a pre-sales Solutions Consultant specializing in fintech software. I work alongside our sales teams throughout the deal cycle, translating technical architecture into business value that resonates with both finance and IT stakeholders.


Over the past eight years, I've driven customer value across the payments and spend management landscape. At Emburse, I focused on Travel & Expense, Business Spend Management, and Corporate Card programs. At Elavon, I specialized in integrated embedded payments and B2B corporate card solutions. At Billtrust and Coupa, I worked in eInvoicing and Procure-to-Pay.


I'm most proud of the work solving customer challenges within the payments ecosystem such as reimbursement workflows and card programs at Emburse, and embedded payment solutions at Elavon. A consistent thread across all of it: deep focus on integrating with client's financial systems of record (ERP), since that's usually where the real business case gets made or lost.


My strength is getting prospects across the bridge, connecting technical capability to the financial metrics executives care about, and code-switching fluently between finance and IT audiences in the same room. That means building demos that don't just show features, but let clients envision the full potential of the solution in their own environment.


Day to day, I deliver strategic functional SaaS demos, articulate solutions architecture and application security, and translate integration strategy, such as flat-file exchange via sFTP, and connectivity via REST API, into language that lands with both technical and business buyers.
